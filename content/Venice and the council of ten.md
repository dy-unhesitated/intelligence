In the late 16th century, Venice was split. The _Vecchi_ (old) wanted to stay Catholic and Mediterranean-focused. The **Giovani (The Young)** wanted to pivot.

- **The Strategy:** The _Giovani_ realized that Venice was physically trapped in the Adriatic. To survive the "Laws of Motion," they needed to move their capital and influence into the rising Protestant powers (the Dutch and English) who were the enemies of their enemies (the Spanish Habsburgs).
    
- **The Link:** Venetian diplomats and bankers began moving their "syndicate" techniques—double-entry bookkeeping, maritime insurance, and intelligence networks—to **Amsterdam**.
