**Protectiveness:** True growth emerges naturally under the right conditions. It is our duty to guard these grassroots emergences from exploitation. Rejecting the ambition of conquest, we instead extend a roof over the natural, shielding it from the jealous and the greedy. Shielding by our guiding principles and harmonizing the fluxing elements. Let the ill-spirited cast fire into our waters, water upon our earth or march their metals trough our fire pits; justice will be served.
